| *ref: FAST-STREAMING (051721Z)* | *0.89–0.93* | *0.13–0.32 s* | *~89–96 ms* | *96–100%* | *0.43–0.88 / 1.37–1.86 / 5.84 s (48.7 s clip)* | – | – |

What these numbers show:
- **Throughput is unchanged and still below real-time.** Steady state is about 0.91× (RTF ≈ 1.10–1.15) at full SM clock. The clock held at 2515–2561 MHz throughout both perf runs, so throttling is not the cause.
- **Jitter has changed shape under the lean loop.** The median frame interval is now about 80 ms, compared with about 89 ms before. Roughly half the frames now arrive on time, compared with almost none before. But frame p95 roughly doubled, from about 93 ms to about 190 ms. Frames now arrive in bursts with occasional double-frame gaps.
- **The prebuffer a client needs for gapless playback is about 9–11% of clip length.** That is about 0.8 s for a 5 s clip, 1.3–1.8 s for 12–14 s, and 3.3–4.2 s for 30–38 s, which matches the steady-state deficit. TTFA stays around 0.25 s.

## Service status

Breeze TTS 2 on goldielocks (fast_mode=full, lean loop) is **functionally green, with no regressions against FAST-20261007**. All 230 shared cases match the FAST results. Design, clone, direction, saved voices, the voice changer, tags, inline direction, speed, the WAV/PCM formats, streaming semantics (abort, 409, lock wait) and every guard and error path pass. Every remaining failure is a known item:
- seed-dependent emotion and tag outcomes
- first-word mangling for angry at cfg 4
- the limiter, which can't be proven through the model
- seeds that are not bit-identical
- dual CFG returning 400, as expected in fast mode
- two stale harness expectations (`long_xl_design`, `err_missing_text`)

**Performance is stable but not real-time.** Steady state is 0.91× (three runs: 0.910, 0.918, 0.908) at a steady 2.5 GHz SM clock. TTFA is about 0.25 s, frame p95 is about 190 ms, and gapless streaming needs a client prebuffer of about 10% of the clip length.
